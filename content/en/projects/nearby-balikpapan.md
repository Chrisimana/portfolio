## Overview

NearBy Balikpapan is a web platform that helps residents discover and learn
about micro, small, and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Balikpapan, East
Kalimantan, Indonesia. It presents location-based information about local
businesses to support the digital transformation of small business owners.

## My Role

This was a team project, an academic initiative by Institut Teknologi
Kalimantan students. I worked as the Front-End Developer and UI/UX
Designer: shaping the interface design and user flows, translating them
into responsive Vue components, and building the catalog, search, filtering,
and business-profile pages on the client side.

## Problem

Many SMEs in Balikpapan lack an easily discoverable digital presence, while
residents struggle to find nearby local businesses,
because the information is scattered and not centralized.

## Solution

NearBy Balikpapan brings an SME catalog into a single searchable, filterable
directory, with location-based discovery to surface nearby businesses,
detailed business profiles, and a recommendation system. My design focus was
making search feel fast and keeping the layout clear on small screens.

## Features

- SME catalog with search and category filtering
- Location-based discovery of nearby businesses
- Detailed business profile pages
- Business recommendation system
- Mobile-responsive interface

## Tech Stack

On the front end I used Vue 3 (Composition API) and TypeScript on Vite, with
Tailwind CSS for styling and Pinia for state management. I did the interface
design and user-flow prototyping before implementation.

## Challenges

Aligning the design with real data from a still-evolving backend, and keeping
search and filtering responsive as the number of registered businesses
grows.

## What I Learned

This project exercised cross-role teamwork: handing over designs that could
be implemented directly, agreeing on an API contract with the backend team,
and keeping UI components consistent as features kept being added.

## Result

NearBy Balikpapan is live at `nearbybalikpapan.com` with a growing set of
businesses, categories, and district coverage, and remains under active
development.
